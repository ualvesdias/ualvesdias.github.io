ZERO TRUST (in Portuguese: Confiança Zero)
A puzzle game about physical penetration testing.

Developer:      Ulisses Alves (one person; a first game)
Release:        14 January 2027
Platforms:      iPhone and iPad (App Store). An Android version is in preparation.
App Store name: Zero Trust: Pentest Puzzle
Price:          Free, with ads between jobs and never during one. One purchase (US$1.99) removes the ads.
Languages:      English, Portuguese (Brazil)
Genre:          Stealth puzzle, one player, plays offline
Website:        https://ualvesdias.github.io/zero-trust/
Press contact:  odisseualves@gmail.com

Zero Trust is a puzzle game about a job few people know exists: physical penetration testing. Companies hire you to break
into their own buildings, reach the server and walk out unseen, so they can learn how a real intruder would do it.
Each of its 56 jobs is a building, or a whole fenced site of them, seen from above in pixel art. Rooms stay dark until you
step into them. Guards walk their rounds, cameras sweep, presence sensors bring the guards, and the receptionist decides
who gets in. You pick locks with a timing game, talk your way past the front desk, and read sticky notes and unlocked
screens for passwords. Every job is authorized by the client in the story: it is a game, not a manual.

Zero Trust: privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026.

Zero Trust (in Portuguese, Confiança Zero) is a game for iPhone, iPad and Android by Ulisses Alves. The game is free and shows advertising. A one-time purchase inside the game removes the advertising.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts and asks for no name, e-mail address or any other detail about you. It does not read your contacts, your location, your photos, your microphone or your camera. The developer receives no personal information about players.

Advertising

Between jobs the game may show a full-screen advertisement. The advertisements are supplied by Unity (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads) and by Google (AdMob).

  • The game asks these companies for non-personalised advertisements: advertisements that are not chosen from a profile of what you do in other apps and websites. The game does not ask for permission to track you.
  • To deliver an advertisement, count it and prevent fraud, these companies receive technical information from your device, such as its IP address, the model and system version, the language, an identifier of the device or of the app installation where your device makes one available, and whether an advertisement was shown or tapped. They handle that information under their own privacy policies:
    • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
    • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game shows a consent message before any advertisement. You can change your choice later from the "No ads" panel on the title screen.
  • Without an internet connection the game shows no advertisements and works in full.

The purchase that removes advertising

The purchase is handled by the store of your device, the App Store or Google Play. The game never sees your payment details. It only learns from the store that the purchase was made, and remembers that on your device so that it shows no more advertisements. The game talks to the store through Unity's purchasing software, and Unity may receive a record that the purchase took place, tied to an identifier of the app installation. Unity handles it under its privacy policy, linked above.

What stays on your device

Your progress in the game (the jobs you finished, your stars, your findings, your gear, the language and the sound setting, and whether advertising was removed) is saved in a file on your own device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game. If your device makes backups, for example to iCloud or to a computer, that file may be part of them; the game has no access to those backups.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and its advertising is not meant for them.

Changes to this policy

If a later version of the game changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
